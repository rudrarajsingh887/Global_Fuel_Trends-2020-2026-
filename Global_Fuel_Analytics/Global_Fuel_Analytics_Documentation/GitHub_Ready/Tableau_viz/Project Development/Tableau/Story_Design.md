# Tableau Story Design

The Story contains seven scenes and is published separately from the dashboard.

| Scene | Purpose | Content |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction — problem / research question | Explain motivation, scope and analytical question. |
| 2 | Geographic disparity | Compare country/region fuel-price variation using maps. |
| 3 | Temporal trends | Review petrol, diesel, LPG and crude-indicator trends. |
| 4 | Taxation policy | Compare regional tax-percentage averages. |
| 5 | Income-level comparison | Compare fuel-price averages across income groups. |
| 6 | Subsidy assessment | Compare averages by subsidy category; avoid causal claims. |
| 7 | Conclusions and recommendations | Summarize patterns and next analytical steps. |

The introduction and conclusion use concise text to avoid duplicating charts. The owner confirmed that all seven scenes were navigable in the browser.
