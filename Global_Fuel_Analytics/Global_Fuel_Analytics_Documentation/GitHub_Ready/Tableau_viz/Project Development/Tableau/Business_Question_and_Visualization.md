# Business Questions and Visualizations

| # | Business question | Tableau visualization / view | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do petrol prices vary by country? | Geographic petrol-price map | Compare spatial distribution. |
| 2 | How do diesel prices vary by country? | Geographic diesel-price map | Compare diesel levels. |
| 3 | How do petrol prices change over time? | Petrol line chart | Inspect time trends. |
| 4 | How do diesel prices change over time? | Diesel line chart | Compare the diesel trend. |
| 5 | How do LPG prices change over time? | LPG line chart | Inspect LPG variation. |
| 6 | How does Brent crude change over time? | Brent crude line chart | Provide context for the energy-price indicator. |
| 7 | How does Brent crude move alongside petrol? | Brent crude vs petrol comparison | Examine co-movement without claiming causation. |
| 8 | How does average tax percentage vary by region? | Regional tax comparison / pie chart | Compare regional indicators. |
| 9 | Which countries have high petrol prices? | Top-countries bar chart | Rank countries in the selected state. |
| 10 | How does price vary by subsidy level? | Fuel price by subsidy level | Compare subsidy categories descriptively. |
| 11 | Are country-to-region mappings consistent? | Region Mapping Check | Identify mapping flags. |
| 12 | Are there repeated country-date keys? | Country-Date Record Count and Duplicate Country-Date Flag | Identify potential duplicate records. |
